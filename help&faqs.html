Help & FAQs

📅 How do I make a booking?

Making a booking is easy! Follow these steps:

  1. Login or Sign up for an account
  2. Go to the "Book" page
  3. Select your preferred property and city
  4. Choose your check-in and check-out dates
  5. Enter number of guests
  6. Select payment method (Credit Card, MTN MoMo, or Airtel Money)
  7. Click "Complete Booking & Pay"
  8. You will receive a confirmation via WhatsApp and Email

🔑 What do I need to check-in?

For check-in, please bring:

  • 🪪 Valid ID/Passport
  • 📱 Booking confirmation (digital or printed)
  • 💰 Security deposit ($50 USD)
  • 📝 Any special request confirmations

⏰ What are check-in and check-out times?

Standard Times:

  • Check-in: 2:00 PM (14:00)
  • Check-out: 11:00 AM (11:00)

Early Check-in: Available from 10:00 AM for $20

Late Check-out: Available until 2:00 PM for $20

💰 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

  • 💳 Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard)
  • 📱 MTN Mobile Money
  • 📱 Airtel Money
  • 💵 Cash (USD or UGX at check-in)

💵 Is there a security deposit?

Yes, a security deposit of $50 USD is required at check-in.

It covers damages, missing items, or excessive cleaning. Fully refundable at check-out after inspection.

❌ What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation Policy:

  • ✅ Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in - 100% refund
  • ⚠️ 50% refund up to 48 hours before check-in
  • ❌ No refund within 48 hours of check-in
  • 💰 $25 cancellation fee applies to all cancellations

🔄 Can I modify my booking dates?

Yes, you can modify your booking dates subject to availability. Contact us at least 48 hours before check-in.

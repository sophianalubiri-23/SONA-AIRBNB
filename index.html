Help & FAQs

📅 How do I make a booking?

Making a booking is easy! Follow these steps:

  1. Login or Sign up for an account (top right corner)
  2. Go to the "Book" page from the navigation menu
  3. Select your preferred property and city from the dropdown
  4. Choose your check-in and check-out dates (minimum 1 night)
  5. Enter number of guests (maximum varies by property)
  6. Review the price breakdown (nightly rate, cleaning fee, service fee, tax)
  7. Select payment method (Credit Card, MTN MoMo, or Airtel Money)
  8. Enter payment details (card number, expiry, CVC for credit cards)
  9. Click "Complete Booking & Pay"
  10. You will receive a confirmation via WhatsApp and Email with your unique Booking ID

You can view all your bookings in the "My Bookings" section. Need help? Contact us on WhatsApp: 0791825920

🔑 What do I need to check-in?

For check-in, please bring the following:

  • 🪪 Valid ID/Passport (for identification - required for all guests)
  • 📱 Booking confirmation (digital or printed copy of your Booking ID)
  • 💰 Security deposit ($50 USD) - can be paid in cash or card
  • 📝 Any special request confirmations (airport pickup, early check-in, etc.)

Check-in time: 2:00 PM (14:00)
Early check-in: Available from 10:00 AM for $20 (subject to availability)
Late check-in: Available 24/7 with prior arrangement

Please contact us at least 24 hours before arrival if you will be checking in outside normal hours.

⏰ What are check-in and check-out times?

Standard Times:

  • Check-in: 2:00 PM (14:00)
  • Check-out: 11:00 AM (11:00)

Early Check-in:

  • Available from 10:00 AM for $20
  • Subject to availability (room must be ready)
  • Request at least 24 hours in advance

Late Check-out:

  • Available until 2:00 PM for $20
  • Subject to availability (no incoming bookings)
  • Request by 9:00 AM on check-out day

Luggage Storage: Free before check-in and after check-out

Late check-out without approval will incur a charge of $50.

👥 What is the maximum occupancy?

Maximum guests per property:

  • Kampala Executive Suite: 4 guests
  • Jinja Riverside Cottage: 6 guests
  • Entebbe Airport Penthouse: 5 guests
  • Mbarara Modern Studio: 3 guests
  • Gulu Peace Haven: 4 guests

⚠️ Exceeding the maximum occupancy without prior approval will result in:

  • Additional $50 per extra guest per night
  • Possible cancellation of booking without refund

Children under 2 years stay free. Extra beds available for $20 per night.

💰 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept the following payment methods:

  • 💳 Credit/Debit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express (secure online payment)
  • 📱 MTN Mobile Money: Pay directly from your MTN account using MoMo
  • 📱 Airtel Money: Pay directly from your Airtel account
  • 💵 Cash: Accepted at check-in (USD or UGX at current exchange rate)
  • 🏦 Bank Transfer: Available for corporate bookings (contact us for details)

Important Notes:

  • All online payments are 100% secure and encrypted
  • Currency conversion fees may apply for international cards
  • Payments are processed in USD, UGX, EUR, or GBP (select your preferred currency at the top of the website)

Yes, a security deposit of $50 USD is required at check-in.

What it covers:

  • Damaged furniture, appliances, or fixtures
  • Missing items (towels, linens, electronics, remote controls, etc.)

  • Violation of house rules (smoking inside, unauthorized guests, etc.)

Refund Process:

  • Deposit is fully refundable at check-out after room inspection
  • Refunds processed within 7 business days
  • Refund sent via same payment method used for deposit
  • Itemized deductions will be provided if any damages found

If no damages or issues are found, you will receive your full deposit back.

You can pay in four different currencies:
